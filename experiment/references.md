### Textbooks

**1. Error Control Coding**<br/>
BY: S. Lin and D. Costello - 2nd Edition, Prentice-Hall, 2004<br/>
_Comprehensive coverage of Hamming codes, single-error correction, extended Hamming codes, and syndrome decoding._

**2. The Theory of Error-Correcting Codes**<br/>
BY: F. MacWilliams and N. Sloane - North-Holland Publishing Company, 1977<br/>
_Classic reference on algebraic coding theory with detailed treatment of Hamming codes and their properties._

**3. Fundamentals of Error-Correcting Codes**<br/>
BY: W. Huffman and V. Pless - Cambridge University Press, 2003<br/>
_Accessible introduction to Hamming codes, perfect codes, and their decoding algorithms._

**4. A First Course in Coding Theory**<br/>
BY: Raymond Hill - Oxford University Press, 1986<br/>
_Clear explanations of Hamming code construction, parity check matrices, and error detection capabilities._

**5. Introduction to Coding Theory**<br/>
BY: J. H. van Lint - Springer, 1999<br/>
_Mathematical foundations of Hamming codes including distance properties and optimality._
